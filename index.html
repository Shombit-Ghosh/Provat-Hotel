About Provat Hotel & Restaurant

Your dependable accommodation and dining address on DBC Road, Jalpaiguri.

Provat Hotel & Restaurant is a dedicated local hospitality destination in Jalpaiguri, designed to cater to daily travelers, visiting officials, families, and food lovers. We bring together essential lodging conveniences and wholesome local dining right in the town's prime commercial zone.

Whether you are stopping by for an authentic Bengali lunch thali, grabbing tasty evening Chinese snacks, or requiring an economical and restful room for the night, we strive to make your visit easy and enjoyable.
